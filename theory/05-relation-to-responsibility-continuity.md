# Relation to Responsibility Continuity

Instant Anomaly Governance naturally connects to Responsibility Continuity because responsibility states can also experience inherited displacement.

A responsibility condition may appear stable at one continuity slice but become unstable if anomaly inheritance redirects continuity.

---

# Responsibility Continuity

Responsibility Continuity refers to whether responsibility remains coherent, attributable, and stable across continuity transitions.

This includes:

- continuity of objectives
- continuity of operational meaning
- continuity of attribution
- continuity of authority conditions
- continuity of consequence paths

Responsibility is therefore not only a point-in-time condition.

It is also a continuity condition.

---

# Shared Governance Boundary

Both systems depend on continuity inheritance.

Responsibility Continuity asks:

Does responsibility remain stable across transitions?

Instant Anomaly Governance asks:

Did a displacement anomaly become inherited into the next continuity slice?

---

# Combined Governance Logic

The relationship can be represented as:

```text
Continuity Slice
→ Responsibility Condition
→ Instant Anomaly Detection
→ Validity Inheritance Check
→ Responsibility Continuity Maintained or Drifted
→ Consequence Transition
```

---

# Responsibility Drift

Responsibility continuity can drift when:

- objectives silently change
- continuity meaning becomes displaced
- authority conditions become unstable
- attribution context changes without explicit transition
- competing continuity paths become inherited

This means responsibility drift is not always caused by a single event.

It may emerge through inherited anomaly accumulation across slices.

---

# Restoration Logic

If anomaly inheritance is corrected before continuity shifts, responsibility continuity can remain stable.

This creates a restoration path before full responsibility drift occurs.

---

# Governance Importance

This matters for:

- AI runtime governance
- human-AI continuity systems
- long-memory interaction systems
- downstream evidence integrity
- continuity-sensitive operational environments

Instant Anomaly Governance therefore acts as an upstream continuity-protection layer for responsibility continuity systems.

---

# Core Principle

Responsibility drift is often inherited before it becomes visible.

# Terminology

This document freezes the core terminology used inside Instant Anomaly Governance.

---

# Logic Slice

A bounded continuity unit where objective, context, operational meaning, and continuity direction are evaluated before the next inheritance transition.

A logic slice is not permanent.

It represents one continuity step.

---

# Instant Anomaly

A momentary displacement pressure appearing inside a continuity slice.

An instant anomaly does not automatically become drift.

It becomes important only if inherited into the next slice.

---

# Validity Inheritance Check

The governance boundary that determines whether the current slice state passes into the next continuity slice unchanged, corrected, restricted, or blocked.

This is the key transition surface of the theory.

---

# Inherited Drift

Drift produced when an anomaly becomes inherited into the next continuity slice and begins redirecting continuity lineage.

Inherited drift is different from a temporary anomaly.

---

# Restoration

The correction or prevention of anomaly inheritance before continuity becomes displaced.

Restoration preserves the original valid continuity path.

---

# Continuity Lineage

The inherited directional chain formed across continuity slices.

Lineage determines whether continuity remains stable, displaced, restored, or drifted over time.

---

# Valid-State Continuity

The long-term preservation of valid operational continuity across changing contexts, responsibilities, and consequence conditions.

Valid-State Continuity is broader than a single valid state.

---

# Responsibility Continuity

The preservation of stable attribution, operational meaning, authority conditions, and consequence alignment across continuity transitions.

Responsibility continuity may drift if anomalies become inherited.

---

# Drift

Inherited displacement across continuity slices.

Drift is not defined by anomaly appearance alone.

Drift begins when displacement becomes continuity lineage.

---

# Core Principle

Anomaly appearance is temporary.

Inheritance determines lineage.

# Why Pre-Drift Governance Matters

Traditional governance systems often detect problems only after drift becomes visible.

By the time drift is observable, continuity lineage may already have shifted.

Instant Anomaly Governance introduces a more upstream governance position.

It focuses on the moment before displacement becomes inherited continuity.

---

# Traditional Drift Detection

Traditional drift governance usually monitors:

- downstream instability
- degraded outputs
- operational divergence
- behavioral inconsistency
- accumulated failure patterns

These approaches are important.

However, they often observe drift after continuity has already been redirected.

---

# Pre-Drift Governance

Instant Anomaly Governance focuses on:

```text
inheritance boundaries
```

instead of only downstream outcomes.

The theory asks:

```text
Did the anomaly become inherited?
```

rather than only:

```text
Did the system already drift?
```

This creates a governance surface earlier than traditional drift detection.

---

# Why Inheritance Matters

A temporary anomaly may disappear naturally.

An inherited anomaly becomes continuity lineage.

This distinction is critical because long-term instability often begins through small inherited displacements rather than sudden catastrophic failures.

---

# Long-Memory Systems

As AI systems become more persistent across time, inherited anomalies may accumulate across many continuity slices.

This can gradually redirect:

- operational meaning
- continuity objectives
- responsibility conditions
- downstream consequence paths

without a single obvious break point.

---

# Human-AI Interaction

The same principle applies to human-AI continuity systems.

A continuity interaction may experience:

- hesitation
- overload
- interruption
- competing objectives
- authority confusion
- pacing instability

These moments are not automatically drift.

The governance question is whether the anomaly becomes inherited continuity.

---

# Governance Shift

This creates a shift from:

```text
post-drift observation
```

to:

```text
pre-drift inheritance governance
```

The governance layer therefore moves closer to continuity formation itself.

---

# Core Principle

Drift is easier to govern before it becomes lineage.

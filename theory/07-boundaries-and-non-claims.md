# Boundaries and Non-Claims

Instant Anomaly Governance is a continuity governance theory.

It defines how momentary anomalies may or may not become inherited continuity drift.

The theory is intentionally bounded.

---

# The Theory Does Not Claim

Instant Anomaly Governance does not claim:

- consciousness detection
- emotion detection
- human psychological diagnosis
- sentience evaluation
- AGI awareness measurement
- legal liability determination
- moral judgment
- truth verification
- predictive certainty
- autonomous authority

The theory only defines continuity inheritance behavior.

---

# Governance Scope

The theory focuses on:

- continuity slices
- anomaly appearance
- inheritance transitions
- continuity displacement
- restoration conditions
- drift formation

Its purpose is governance structure, not human interpretation authority.

---

# Drift Definition Boundary

In this theory:

```text
anomaly ≠ drift
```

Drift begins only when displacement becomes inherited continuity lineage.

This separation is fundamental.

---

# Human and AI Applicability

The theory may be applied to:

- AI runtime systems
- long-memory interaction systems
- operational continuity environments
- human-AI interaction systems
- continuity-sensitive workflows

However, application does not imply psychological interpretation authority.

---

# Engineering Position

Instant Anomaly Governance is designed as:

```text
continuity governance
```

not:

```text
human cognition replacement
```

The purpose is to model continuity inheritance behavior in reproducible governance structures.

---

# Core Boundary Principle

The theory governs continuity inheritance.

It does not claim ownership over consciousness, emotion, identity, or human meaning.
